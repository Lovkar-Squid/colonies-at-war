# Colonies at War 0.3.0 (unreleased)

## Named captains

- **Every raid on a colony is led by one named captain.** MineColonies' barbarians, pirates, Egyptians, Norsemen, Amazons and drowned pirates, a neighbour's warband and a kingdom's men alike: the raid's chief when it brought one, otherwise, after five seconds, its first ordinary fighting man, promoted. One a raid, ever. A chief who arrives later stays a soldier, and a raid whose captain is dead does not get another, a restart included.
- **A name.** Twelve to a list, one list each for barbarians, pirates (the drowned too), Egyptians, Norsemen and Amazons, all in the lang file. A warband's or a kingdom's captain has a plain name and his side's: "Captain Aldric of Ironhold" where 0.2.x said "Captain of Ironhold". The name follows from the raid, and two raids on the same town at once never send two men of the same name.
- **The town is told.** One line to the colony's players when he takes the field ("Thrain Skullsplitter leads the barbarian raid against Ironhold!") and one when he falls. Not when he despawns after a long night: that is not a fall.
- **Better kit, and none of it to keep.** A full set of armour and a weapon a tier above the best his men carry: barbarians with stone axes get a captain in iron. A weapon he already holds that is as good stays, so a barbarian chief keeps his chief sword; a bow, a spear or a scepter stays and is enchanted. Nothing he wears or holds ever drops.
- **More health.** Double what the raid gave him by default, as a modifier, so it survives MineColonies re-statting its raiders when the world loads.
- **A red bar** with his name and his health, for every player within 64 blocks of him. It goes when he dies, despawns or is unloaded or when the raid ends, and comes back with him after a restart: the captain carries his mark and his name in his own saved data.
- **A trophy.** Killed by a player or any colony guard, he drops his raid's banner, named "Banner of <his name>", saying which raid on which colony and the colony's day he fell. A warband's banner is its own colony's flag on its colour. Once per captain: a second death event, or the drops being asked for again, gives nothing. Servers with mob loot off get no trophy either.
- Four new server settings under `captains`: `captains` (on, the default, or off), `captainExtraHealth` (percent, 100), `captainBossBar` (on) and `captainTrophy` (`banner`, or `weapon` for his weapon, named the same way).
- A warband's other men are now named through the lang file too ("Soldier of Ironhold").
- Worlds from 0.2.2 load as they were. A raid already on when the world is loaded gets its captain then.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/capgate`: MineColonies' own barbarian raid started through its raid manager, a warband and a warband without a chief, at once. 44 checks, every one passed: one named captain each, in iron over stone-axe men, at 27, 60 and 40 health on bases of 13.5, 30 and 20, each bar red and following his health down to half; a chief sent late stayed a soldier; killed by a fake player each dropped exactly one named trophy with its lore (the raid, the colony, the colony's day) and none of his armour, the bar went, a second death dropped nothing, and nobody was promoted in his place. The gate sets `captainTrophy` itself before each kill, so one run proves both kinds: the two chiefs left banners, the chiefless warband's captain his iron axe.

## Raid targets from other mods

- **Other mods' camps and villages are targets.** A warband can march on Kings & Monsters' ogre outposts (plains and swamp) and the Ogre King's fort, IFC-Integrations' goblin camp, and Valarian Conquest's bandit camp, bandit ruins, tent camps, the neutral outpost and every faction's outpost and castle; and on a kobold den (Kobolds) or a dwarf village (IFC-Integrations) as a village. None of these mods is needed: each entry in the two structure tags is optional, so a world without the mod has one target fewer and nothing else changes. There is no dependency on any of them, at load or at build.
- Valarian Conquest 4.2.2 has no Bohemian castle (the Bohemians have only an outpost), so that one is not in the tag.
- **A place's defenders are counted by what they do, not by which mod they come from.** A camp counts its armed men: anything hostile, or any mob holding a bow, a crossbow or something that adds to its attack, and a Guard Villagers guard who stands in it. A village counts its iron golems, Guard Villagers' guards, its armed men and its folk, the non-hostile people in it (villagers, kobolds, dwarves; not the animals). Each one is counted once, inside the structure's own box as well as the old reach round it, so a large village is counted whole. As before, only when somebody has the place loaded.
- **Names read the structure's own name.** `/war targets` and the War Table say "the Ogre Outpost at ..." or "the Bandit Ruins at ...", not "the Ogre Outpost Plains" or "the Bandit Ruins 2": a trailing biome word or number is dropped.
- **Guard Villagers, fully.** Reached by its entity id only. Every guard in a village adds 12 to its defence by default (an iron golem adds 15, a villager 2); the new server setting `villageGuardWeight` under `campaigns` changes it. A warband that fights a village with guards loses men to them: a man for every guard when it is thrown back, one for every two when it wins, never more than it brought. Its sellswords die first; its own guards are not killed but come home carried, at the end of their strength. Of the village's guards, half and more fall when the warband wins, a quarter at most when it is thrown back. The battle report says it: "was thrown back (60 +70 against 165); 10 village guards stood against it and 2 fell; it lost 3 men (1 sellsword killed, 2 guards carried home)".
- The campaign keeps how many guards the village had, how many fell, how many men the warband lost and how many came home carried, for the village's answer in a later part of 0.3.0. Worlds from 0.2.2 read all four as 0.
- Plunder from a modded camp is `plunder/camp`, from a modded village `plunder/village`: our loot tables name no other mod's items.
- A new static check, `tools/check_tags.py`, reads every other mod's id in the two tags back out of that mod's jar and fails on a typo.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/targetgate`, with Guard Villagers 2.4.12, Kobolds 4.0.2 and Valarian Conquest 4.2.2 loaded. 25 checks, every one passed: the camp tag resolved 26 structures, every Valarian camp, outpost and castle among them, and the village tag the kobold den and the vanilla villages; a vanilla village placed by hand read as "Village", with 6 guards Guard Villagers had put there itself, one golem and 9 villagers, defence 117; four more guards spawned took it to 165, 12 apiece, and changed nothing else in the count; a Valarian bandit camp and the bandit ruins read as camps named "Bandit Camp" and "Bandit Ruins"; in the bandit camp a villager handed an iron sword, an empty-handed vindicator and a guard each added 3 to its defence (20 to 29), a cow nothing; a warband of two guards and a sellsword sent at the guarded village was thrown back, recorded 10 guards and 2 fallen (1 in a second run, the luck being worse), lost its sellsword and brought its two guards home carried; all four numbers came back the same from a save, and as 0 from a save without them. Removing the guard count made five of the checks fail.

Not proven on the rig: IFC-Integrations 4.0.1 does not load without Minecraft Comes Alive (it asks for MCA's villager class while loading, though its mod file does not say so), and Kings & Monsters needs GeckoLib and more. Their structures (goblin camp, dwarf village, the ogre camps) are checked against their jars by `tools/check_tags.py` only. The kobold den is in the tag and resolved on the rig, but could not be placed there: its own structure type finds no spot for itself on a flat world.
