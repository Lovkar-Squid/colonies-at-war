# Colonies at War 0.3.0 (unreleased)

## Named captains

- **Every raid on a colony is led by one named captain.** MineColonies' barbarians, pirates, Egyptians, Norsemen, Amazons and drowned pirates, a neighbour's warband and a kingdom's men alike: the raid's chief when it brought one, otherwise, after five seconds, its first ordinary fighting man, promoted. One a raid, ever. A chief who arrives later stays a soldier, and a raid whose captain is dead does not get another, a restart included.
- **A name.** Twelve to a list, one list each for barbarians, pirates (the drowned too), Egyptians, Norsemen and Amazons, all in the lang file. A warband's or a kingdom's captain has a plain name and his side's: "Captain Aldric of Ironhold" where 0.2.x said "Captain of Ironhold". The name follows from the raid, and two raids on the same town at once never send two men of the same name.
- **The town is told.** One line to the colony's players when he takes the field ("Thrain Skullsplitter leads the barbarian raid against Ironhold!") and one when he falls. Not when he despawns after a long night: that is not a fall.
- **Better kit, and none of it to keep.** A full set of armour and a weapon a tier above the best his men carry: barbarians with stone axes get a captain in iron. A weapon he already holds that is as good stays, so a barbarian chief keeps his chief sword; a bow, a spear or a scepter stays and is enchanted. Nothing he wears or holds ever drops.
- **More health.** Double what the raid gave him by default, as a modifier, so it survives MineColonies re-statting its raiders when the world loads.
- **A red bar** with his name and his health, for every player within 64 blocks of him. It goes when he dies, despawns or is unloaded or when the raid ends, and comes back with him after a restart: the captain carries his mark and his name in his own saved data.
- **A trophy.** Killed by a player or any colony guard, he drops his raid's banner, named "Banner of <his name>", saying which raid on which colony and the colony's day he fell. A warband's banner is its own colony's flag on its colour. Once per captain: a second death event, or the drops being asked for again, gives nothing. Servers with mob loot off get no trophy either.
- Four new server settings under `captains`: `captains` (on, the default, or off), `captainExtraHealth` (percent, 100), `captainBossBar` (on) and `captainTrophy` (`banner`, or `weapon` for his weapon, named the same way).
- A warband's other men are now named through the lang file too ("Soldier of Ironhold").
- Worlds from 0.2.2 load as they were. A raid already on when the world is loaded gets its captain then.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/capgate`: MineColonies' own barbarian raid started through its raid manager, a warband and a warband without a chief, at once. 44 checks, every one passed: one named captain each, in iron over stone-axe men, at 27, 60 and 40 health on bases of 13.5, 30 and 20, each bar red and following his health down to half; a chief sent late stayed a soldier; killed by a fake player each dropped exactly one named trophy with its lore (the raid, the colony, the colony's day) and none of his armour, the bar went, a second death dropped nothing, and nobody was promoted in his place. The gate sets `captainTrophy` itself before each kill, so one run proves both kinds: the two chiefs left banners, the chiefless warband's captain his iron axe.
