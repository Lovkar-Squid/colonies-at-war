# Colonies at War 0.3.0 (17 Nov 2026)

## Named captains

- **Every raid on a colony is led by one named captain.** MineColonies' barbarians, pirates, Egyptians, Norsemen, Amazons and drowned pirates, a neighbour's warband and a kingdom's men alike: the raid's chief when it brought one, otherwise, after five seconds, its first ordinary fighting man, promoted. One a raid, ever. A chief who arrives later stays a soldier, and a raid whose captain is dead does not get another, a restart included.
- **A name.** Twelve to a list, one list each for barbarians, pirates (the drowned too), Egyptians, Norsemen and Amazons, all in the lang file. A warband's or a kingdom's captain has a plain name and his side's: "Captain Aldric of Ironhold" where 0.2.x said "Captain of Ironhold". The name follows from the raid, and two raids on the same town at once never send two men of the same name.
- **The town is told.** One line to the colony's players when he takes the field ("Thrain Skullsplitter leads the barbarian raid against Ironhold!") and one when he falls. Not when he despawns after a long night: that is not a fall.
- **Better kit, and none of it to keep.** A full set of armour and a weapon a tier above the best his men carry: barbarians with stone axes get a captain in iron. A weapon he already holds that is as good stays, so a barbarian chief keeps his chief sword; a bow, a spear or a scepter stays and is enchanted. Nothing he wears or holds ever drops.
- **More health.** Double what the raid gave him by default, as a modifier, so it survives MineColonies re-statting its raiders when the world loads.
- **A red bar** with his name and his health, for every player within 64 blocks of him. It goes when he dies, despawns or is unloaded or when the raid ends, and comes back with him after a restart: the captain carries his mark and his name in his own saved data.
- **A trophy.** Killed by a player or any colony guard, he drops his raid's banner, named "Banner of <his name>", saying which raid on which colony and the colony's day he fell. A warband's banner is its own colony's flag on its colour. Once per captain: a second death event, or the drops being asked for again, gives nothing. Servers with mob loot off get no trophy either.
- **Names that let them fight.** A captain's name and his men's names hang over their heads as plain text, translated on every client. A name kept as a translation key would have been read by MineColonies' raider AI as the name of a kind of damage, and every blow the man swung would have failed; the in-game test caught that before release.
- Four new server settings under `captains`: `captains` (on, the default, or off), `captainExtraHealth` (percent, 100), `captainBossBar` (on) and `captainTrophy` (`banner`, or `weapon` for his weapon, named the same way).
- A warband's other men are now named through the lang file too ("Soldier of Ironhold").
- Worlds from 0.2.2 load as they were. A raid already on when the world is loaded gets its captain then.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/capgate`: MineColonies' own barbarian raid started through its raid manager, a warband and a warband without a chief, at once. 44 checks, every one passed: one named captain each, in iron over stone-axe men, at 27, 60 and 40 health on bases of 13.5, 30 and 20, each bar red and following his health down to half; a chief sent late stayed a soldier; killed by a fake player each dropped exactly one named trophy with its lore (the raid, the colony, the colony's day) and none of his armour, the bar went, a second death dropped nothing, and nobody was promoted in his place. The gate sets `captainTrophy` itself before each kill, so one run proves both kinds: the two chiefs left banners, the chiefless warband's captain his iron axe.

## Raid targets from other mods

- **Other mods' camps and villages are targets.** A warband can march on Kings & Monsters' ogre outposts (plains and swamp) and the Ogre King's fort, IFC-Integrations' goblin camp, and Valarian Conquest's bandit camp, bandit ruins, tent camps, the neutral outpost and every faction's outpost and castle; and on a kobold den (Kobolds) or a dwarf village (IFC-Integrations) as a village. None of these mods is needed: each entry in the two structure tags is optional, so a world without the mod has one target fewer and nothing else changes. There is no dependency on any of them, at load or at build.
- Valarian Conquest 4.2.2 has no Bohemian castle (the Bohemians have only an outpost), so that one is not in the tag.
- **A place's defenders are counted by what they do, not by which mod they come from.** A camp counts its armed men: anything hostile, or any mob holding a bow, a crossbow or something that adds to its attack, and a Guard Villagers guard who stands in it. A village counts its iron golems, Guard Villagers' guards, its armed men and its folk, the non-hostile people in it (villagers, kobolds, dwarves; not the animals). Each one is counted once, inside the structure's own box as well as the old reach round it, so a large village is counted whole. As before, only when somebody has the place loaded. A colony's own people standing beside a camp or a village (a town built next to a village, say) are never counted as its defenders.
- **Names read the structure's own name.** `/war targets` and the War Table say "the Ogre Outpost at ..." or "the Bandit Ruins at ...", not "the Ogre Outpost Plains" or "the Bandit Ruins 2": a trailing biome word or number is dropped.
- **Guard Villagers, fully.** Reached by its entity id only. Every guard in a village adds 12 to its defence by default (an iron golem adds 15, a villager 2); the new server setting `villageGuardWeight` under `campaigns` changes it. A warband that fights a village with guards loses men to them: a man for every guard when it is thrown back, one for every two when it wins, never more than it brought. Its sellswords die first; its own guards are not killed but come home carried, at the end of their strength. Of the village's guards, half and more fall when the warband wins, a quarter at most when it is thrown back. The battle report says it: "was thrown back (60 +70 against 165); 10 village guards stood against it and 2 fell; it lost 3 men (1 sellsword killed, 2 guards carried home)".
- The campaign keeps how many guards the village had, how many fell, how many men the warband lost and how many came home carried, for the village's answer in a later part of 0.3.0. Worlds from 0.2.2 read all four as 0.
- Plunder from a modded camp is `plunder/camp`, from a modded village `plunder/village`: our loot tables name no other mod's items.
- A new static check, `tools/check_tags.py`, reads every other mod's id in the two tags back out of that mod's jar and fails on a typo.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/targetgate`, with Guard Villagers 2.4.12, Kobolds 4.0.2 and Valarian Conquest 4.2.2 loaded. 25 checks, every one passed: the camp tag resolved 26 structures, every Valarian camp, outpost and castle among them, and the village tag the kobold den and the vanilla villages; a vanilla village placed by hand read as "Village", with 6 guards Guard Villagers had put there itself, one golem and 9 villagers, defence 117; four more guards spawned took it to 165, 12 apiece, and changed nothing else in the count; a Valarian bandit camp and the bandit ruins read as camps named "Bandit Camp" and "Bandit Ruins"; in the bandit camp a villager handed an iron sword, an empty-handed vindicator and a guard each added 3 to its defence (20 to 29), a cow nothing; a warband of two guards and a sellsword sent at the guarded village was thrown back, recorded 10 guards and 2 fallen (1 in a second run, the luck being worse), lost its sellsword and brought its two guards home carried; all four numbers came back the same from a save, and as 0 from a save without them. Removing the guard count made five of the checks fail.

Not proven on the rig: IFC-Integrations 4.0.1 does not load without Minecraft Comes Alive (it asks for MCA's villager class while loading, though its mod file does not say so), and Kings & Monsters needs GeckoLib and more. Their structures (goblin camp, dwarf village, the ogre camps) are checked against their jars by `tools/check_tags.py` only. The kobold den is in the tag and resolved on the rig, but could not be placed there: its own structure type finds no spot for itself on a flat world.

## Convoys on the road

- **A convoy can be robbed on the road.** Colonist Thieves can now see a Colonies at War convoy passing near its Hideout and send a man after it; this part is the Colonies at War side. A convoy is on the road from the moment it leaves until it arrives, on the straight line from the sender's War Room to the colony or kingdom it is going to, as far along as the time since it left says.
- **Never more than half.** A thief takes some of the convoy's stacks, or part of one, and never more than half of what the convoy set out with, however many times it is robbed. A convoy that has arrived, or is coming home, cannot be robbed.
- **Only what is left arrives.** The receiver gets the rest. The war log line says so ("... with 80 item(s) - Iron Ingot (robbed on the road: 80 of its 160 items taken) - standing now 8"), so does the convoy's report, and both colonies are told. A robbed convoy that is called back says so in its report too. Standing is not changed by a robbery: the thief's own mod lowers it when his man is caught or seen.
- **The bridge.** `WarBridge` has four new methods for this: `convoysNear`, `convoyCargo`, `convoyEscort` and `robConvoy`. The six it had are unchanged, so an older Colonist Thieves keeps working, and a Colonist Thieves that looks for the new ones finds no convoys in an older Colonies at War.
- Worlds from 0.2.2 load as they were. A convoy already on the road counts as not robbed, and as having set out with what it carries.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/roadgate`: two colonies 400 blocks apart, each with a War Room, the receiver with a warehouse; two knights hired, 128 iron and 32 bread put in the room and on the manifest, and the convoy sent the way the War Table sends it. 37 checks, every one passed: a quarter of the way into the trip the bridge found it at 115, 0, 8, a quarter of the road, and half way at 213, 0, 8, the point half way between the War Room and the receiver, and did not find it from the War Room, from the receiver or from 60 blocks off the road with a range of 50; its cargo and its escort of two read back right, and the cargo it handed out was a copy; robbed for one stack it gave up the 32 bread, robbed again for ten it stopped at 48 iron, exactly half of the 160 together, and a third time it gave nothing; the theft came back the same from a save mid-road, and a save without it read as not robbed; on arrival the receiver had 80 iron, the report and the war log line said "robbed on the road: 80 of its 160 items taken", standing had not moved until the delivery raised it, and the six older bridge methods all still answered. Letting a robbery take everything made seven of the checks fail, and forgetting the theft when the save is read made one.

## Camps and villages strike back

- **A raided camp or village answers.** A warband that marches on a camp or a village, whether it wins or is thrown back, leaves a debt: some days later the place's survivors come for the colony as a real raid, "Raiders of the Pillager Outpost" from a camp, "Militia of the Village" from a village, led by a captain of their own ("Captain Edmund of the Pillager Outpost leads the raiders of the Pillager Outpost against Ironhold!"). They come after `reprisalDays` (2 by default), in half the time after a sack, like a kingdom's men.
- **A bigger raid, a bigger answer.** How many come is an eighth of the place's defence, one more for each spawner smashed, for every two plunder rolls a sack carried off and for each of its guards who fell, and two for each of its guards who lived through it; never fewer than 3 or more than 16, as for a kingdom. A second attack on the same place before it has answered makes the same debt bigger and nearer, never a second raid.
- **Guard Villagers' guards lead their village's answer.** With Guard Villagers installed, the guards a village had and lost (counted since the previous part) make the core of its militia, and its men come in iron armour with shields; their captain, a tier above them, in diamond. None of it drops.
- **Only when somebody is home.** As with a kingdom, the raid comes only when the colony is active and loaded and sieges are fought in the world (`siegeInWorld`); a debt nobody was home for during a week after it fell due is forgotten. The new server setting `placesStrikeBack` under `campaigns` (on) turns all of it off.
- **Where it shows.** `/war targets` ends with what is owed ("owed: the Pillager Outpost will come for you in 1 day - 9 soldiers"), `/war status` lists it with the kingdoms' debts, and the War Table writes the same line under the place; a place that owes the colony and is not the nearest of its kind gets a line of its own. A kingdom's debt is now written under the kingdom too.
- The night ends as a kingdom's raid does: if half its men still stand at dawn, they carry goods out of the warehouses and break the wall on the side they came from.
- Worlds from 0.2.2 load as they were: a debt saved without a kind is a kingdom's, and kingdom reprisals work as before.

Proven on the headless rig with `tools/revengegate`, with Guard Villagers 2.4.12 loaded: a pillager outpost with two spawners set into it and two plains villages placed by hand, a colony with somebody home and one nobody ever visits. 40 checks, every one passed. Sacked (90 against 30), the outpost had its spawners smashed and owed 9 soldiers in one day; thrown back from a village with Guard Villagers' own guards taken out (defence 47), the colony owed it 6 in two days; thrown back from a village with 10 guards (161), 16, all 10 of them at the head. A second attack on the outpost made its debt 10, the same record and the same day; the other colony's attack on it a debt of its own. All four came back the same from a save, and a kingdom's debt built by hand saved as a kingdom's and read back the same, with its new tags and without them. The War Table said "the Pillager Outpost will come for you in 1 day" and "the Village will come for you in 2 days" twice. With the clock pushed past the due days, the three debts of the colony with somebody home were paid as "Raiders of the Pillager Outpost" (10 men) and two "Militia of the Village" (6 and 16), each with exactly one captain ("Captain Edmund of the Pillager Outpost"), the guarded village's 15 men in iron with shields and their captain in diamond; the other colony's debt waited, and a week later was forgotten ("the Pillager Outpost never came for ..."). Letting a second attack write a second debt made three of the checks fail. `tools/capgate` (44 checks) and `tools/targetgate` (25) still pass.
